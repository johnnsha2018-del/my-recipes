My Recipes

Scan Grandma’s postcard, share your own family recipe, earn a thank-you reward, and shop every ingredient by aisle.

Try this

  1. On the home screen, tap the Asian Food Week banner, or scan the postcard.
  2. Choose “Add my family recipe”, then Save.
  3. Make it a postcard and share it to unlock $5 off.
  4. Add to list and follow the route to the end.
  5. No app? Try “Open the web form”.
